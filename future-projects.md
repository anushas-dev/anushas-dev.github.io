## ✍️ Current Works in Progress (WIP)

### **Project: The Silent Lens**
*A Deep Dive into Visual Poetry*
I am currently working on a collection that bridges the gap between my photography and my prose. This project explores the "irony" in everyday snapshots—the stories we miss when we blink.

> "A photograph is a poem without words; I am giving those photos their voice back."
