# Welcome to the New Chapter

Today marks a significant milestone in my writing journey. While **Lazy Conundrums** remains my heart and my daily journal, this new site serves as the executive library for my published works—from *Sprouted Up Heart* to the visual melodies of *Sky Album*.

### Why a New Space?
As a writer, my work often feels like a series of "thought alleys." Some are for wandering (the blog), and some are for dwelling (my books). This site is designed to:
* Showcase the full **Bibliography**.
* Host the **Bird Watching Gallery** where my photography meets my poetry.
* Provide a direct bridge for collaborators and readers.

### What’s Next?
I am currently finalizing some "Future Projects" that explore the intersection of resilience and nature. Stay tuned as I populate the gallery with new sightings and the bookshelf with new stories.

> "Poetry is my soul sister; it allows me to convey the feelings that are often left unspoken."

Thank you for being part of this evolution. Feel free to explore the [Gallery](gallery.html) or browse my [Books](books.html).
